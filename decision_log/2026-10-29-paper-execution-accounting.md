# Local-paper execution accounting (schema v3)

## Scope and event boundary

`PaperLedger.record_observed_fill` accepts only a bounded, explicit event with `source_kind=already_observed_local_paper`, a stable `fill_id`, the original intent ID, UTC observation time, positive BTC delta and cumulative BTC quantity, positive USDT/BTC price, and an explicitly supplied nonnegative USDT fee. It requires `fill_reason=manual_reconcile`, a mode and stated assumptions. The new `paper_execution_event.schema.json` describes this record; the older `paper_fill.schema.json` is not repurposed. No price-touch rule or live source produces fills here. A durable local acceptance receipt is required before an ordinary fill; acceptance and ACK remain **not fills**. The event timestamp may predate a cancel request when its fill is recorded while cancellation is pending. A correction encountered after a terminal state is retained as an incident, including when its purported event time is before the receipt, rather than applied to inventory.

This API records a claimed already-observed local-paper event. It does **not** authenticate that claim or authorize generation of a new simulated fill from stale/gapped market data. A future generator needs an explicit independent market-quality and gap policy, observation provenance, conservative fill rules and reject behavior before it can call this recorder. Synthetic fixtures exercise only the ledger contract; they are not production observations or fees.

## Accounting and cancellation

The buy-only BTCUSDT ledger tracks original quantity as `filled + remaining + cancelled`, with uniquely indexed fill IDs across applied fills and incidents. It checks delta against the supplied cumulative total. Identical retries return their prior result; conflicting identity reuse raises an error without changing cash/base or the hash chain. Per-intent remaining cash and risk reservations decrease proportionally with remaining BTC, rounded **up** to 16 decimal places; filled risk exposure retains the released risk portion. Actual USDT cash decreases by `(delta BTC × explicit price) + explicit fee`, while actual BTC base increases by delta. Decimal arithmetic runs in a fixed sufficient local precision, independent of caller context. New-risk admission checks cash and risk budgets; **observation recording is a different boundary**. A well-formed, already-observed fill that would overfill, contradict cumulative quantity, predate local acceptance, exceed the fill-count limit, or violate cash/base/reservation/risk accounting is recorded as a reconciliation incident rather than rolled back as a business rejection. Its raw event, stable identity and reason persist, but *no candidate balance, quantity or reservation is applied*. These are accounting and admission bounds, **not** PnL or a full portfolio risk model.

A cancellation request is an immutable pending transition and does not release a reservation. A fill can be recorded exactly once while pending. Only a confirmation for the same cancel ID at/after its request **and every previously recorded fill timestamp** releases the unfilled remainder; a backdated confirmation is rejected while keeping the pending reservation and fill evidence. Already filled cash/base and risk exposure remain. If the remainder became zero while pending, confirmation finalizes `FILLED`, not `CANCELLED`. A previously unseen fill after `CANCELLED` or `FILLED` is persisted as a bounded reconciliation incident with no inventory change. Any incident blocks *new* risk and cancellation releases until explicit future reconciliation; subsequent new observed fills are recorded as further incidents, not applied to uncertain balances. Identical retries of applied fills or incidents remain idempotent; conflicting reuse of an identity fails visibly. If the incident bound is exhausted, storage fails closed and the object halts rather than deleting evidence. Finite per-intent bounds (16 applied fills and 8 incidents) and the existing database, WAL and ledger limits never prune unresolved evidence.

## Durability, migration and verification

Execution, materialized cash/base/risk, immutable fill/cancel/incident identity rows, and a hash-chained ledger event commit in one writer-fenced SQLite transaction. Account snapshots read totals within one serialized transaction and compute available balances at fixed Decimal precision. An uncertain write or failed rollback halts the object. Reopen checks required append-only guards, ledger chain and event order, reconstructs each execution state and aggregate cash/base/risk, and compares materialized rows and bounds before admitting risk. For accounting-limit incidents, recovery independently replays portfolio cash and reservations in ledger order and confirms the rejected candidate really violated a limit at that point; other incident reasons follow the runtime validation precedence against prior per-intent state. Once a new reasoned incident occurs, recovery rejects subsequent risk reservations, applied fills and cancellation releases, and requires subsequent incidents to carry the unresolved-incident reason. Historical reasonless terminal incidents can still precede already-durable legacy transitions, which this follow-up does not retroactively invalidate. This checks a trusted local database for internal consistency; it is not protection against a privileged rewrite of all database/history bytes or evidence of power-loss durability.

New databases are schema v3. Existing v1/v2 files are **not** upgraded by `open`: `migrate_v1(path)` or `migrate_v2(path)` must be invoked explicitly. Each verifies the original chain, trigger definitions, dispatch rows and reservations, installs v3 tables/guards, backfills initial execution states and changes the version in one transaction, then reopens and verifies v3. This follow-up adds an optional, bounded reason to v3 incident ledger events but changes **no SQLite table or on-disk schema version**; historical reasonless terminal corrections remain readable. Synthetic legacy-file tests cover preservation and rejection of corrupt or already migrated inputs. No operator database was migrated or read for this work.

Remaining gates: authoritative local observation provenance; an independently specified fill-simulation and stale/gapped-data policy; cost/slippage/fee calibration; exposure-aware risk beyond this proportional budget; incident investigation/resolution; and any separate PnL/research evaluation. No strategy selection, exchange sender, network effect, collector operation, or real execution is introduced.
